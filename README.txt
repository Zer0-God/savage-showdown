Savage Showdown V0.7
Upload index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, icon-192.png and icon-512.png to the ROOT of your existing GitHub repository (main). Do not upload the ZIP itself. Leave roster.json and README.md alone. Open the site in Safari. Share > Add to Home Screen. Launch once online before offline use. iOS may not honor landscape lock or vibration.
Controls: slide around the D-pad; diagonals combine horizontal and vertical. Neutral jump = up; directional jump = up+left/right; toward opponent within crossover range = tucked flip. Pause > Settings to adjust controller.
