V0.8: release or reverse horizontal direction in midair to feint; press Block or Down in air to brake. Jump + 1/2/3/4 performs aerial attacks. Hold Down + 2 for launcher, Down + 3/4 for sweep. Grab close to a standing opponent for a shoulder throw. Hold Forward or Back to walk continuously. Hold Low and Block together for crouching guard. Hold a direction and tap an attack for directional moves; tap directions in sequence for specials. Multiple fingers work independently. Combos now play individual timed hits; catalog damage remains provisional. Tap directions then attacks for specials; use F+/B+/D+ then attack for simultaneous notation. EX arms the next move. Keyboard: arrows, J K U I, G grab, E environment, Space block. Training move buttons preview catalog attacks; they do not validate real animation timing.
